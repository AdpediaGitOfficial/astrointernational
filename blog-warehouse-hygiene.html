Warehouse Hygiene Best Practices

Facilities April 18, 2025

A clean warehouse protects products, supports employee wellbeing, and helps businesses meet hygiene and safety requirements. With the right cleaning supplies and regular routines, warehouses and distribution centres can remain safe, organised, and compliant.

Key Practices

  • Create daily, weekly, and monthly cleaning schedules for floors, shelves, equipment, restrooms, and loading areas.
  • Use suitable products such as industrial floor cleaners, disinfectants, microfiber cloths, hand sanitiser, gloves, and waste bags.
  • Clean high-touch surfaces, including forklifts, door handles, scanners, workstations, and machinery.
  • Keep aisles clear, remove waste promptly, and store products safely to prevent contamination and accidents.
  • Provide handwashing stations with liquid soap and disposable towels in convenient locations.
  • Inspect regularly for pests, leaks, mould, dust buildup, and damaged packaging.
  • Train employees in cleaning procedures, personal hygiene, chemical handling, and safe waste disposal.
